binary build can be distributed with the notices its dependencies require.

## Mozilla Public License 2.0

The audio decoding stack is MPL-2.0. That licence is file-level copyleft: it is
satisfied by linking the crates unmodified, which is what happens here, and by
telling you where the source is. None of these files have been modified.

Source: https://github.com/pdeljanov/Symphonia
